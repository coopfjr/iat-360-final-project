SFU Undergraduate Course Repeat Policy
Students may repeat courses according to strict university limits. 
The number of courses which a student may repeat in a degree program is limited to five. 
The number of times a student may repeat any specific course is once. 
Courses completed at Simon Fraser University, for which a student has already received transfer credit from another institution, will count within the five repeats limit.
Normally, the need to satisfy a course prerequisite is not acceptable as a reason for duplication beyond stated limits. Obtaining a prerequisite waiver is usually a reasonable alternative. Raising GPA is not an acceptable reason for duplication beyond the stated limits. 
Students in the Faculty of Communication, Art and Technology (FCAT) who wish to exceed these limits must submit a formal Course Repeat Appeal Form. This request will only be granted if there is no reasonable alternative to repeating the course and there is clear evidence that the student will be successful in the repeated course.
