SFU SIAT Upper Division Course Offerings and Prerequisites (300 and 400 Level)

IAT 309W (Writing Methods for Research): Develops critical thinking and writing strategies adaptable to professional communications in design, media arts and technology. 
Prerequisite: IAT 206W with a minimum grade of C- and completion of 48 units.

IAT 312 (Foundations of Game Design): Explores game design principles and methodologies.
Prerequisite: Minimum of 24 units, including one of MATH 130, MACM 101, MATH 150, MATH 151, MATH 154, or MATH 157, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 313 (Narrative and New Media): Explores interactive narrative creation across digital platforms.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units.

IAT 333 (Interaction Design Methods): Focuses on advanced user research and evaluation methods for interaction design.
Prerequisite: IAT 238 and IAT 201, both with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 334 (Interface Design): Advanced interface design principles for complex systems.
Prerequisite: IAT 238 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 340 (Sound Design): Principles and techniques of sound design for media and interactive systems.
Prerequisite: IAT 202 or IAT 265, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 343 (Animation): Theory and practice of 3D computer animation.
Prerequisite: IAT 265 with a minimum grade of C- and completion of 48 units.

IAT 344 (Moving Images): Advanced video production and post-production techniques.
Prerequisite: IAT 202 with a minimum grade of C- and completion of 48 units.

IAT 351 (Advanced Human-Computer Interaction): Advanced topics in HCI research and practice.
Prerequisite: IAT 201 and IAT 238, both with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 355 (Introduction to Visual Analytics): Techniques for analyzing and visualizing complex data sets.
Prerequisite: IAT 201 and IAT 235, both with a minimum grade of C- and completion of 48 units.

IAT 386 (Directed Studies): Independent study project under the supervision of a faculty member.
Prerequisite: Completion of 45 units, and permission of the instructor and of the school. No more than two directed studies may be used towards the IAT major requirements.

IAT 410 (Advanced Game Design): Advanced game development, focusing on complex systems and mechanics.
Prerequisite: IAT 312 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 445 (Immersive Environments): Design and development of virtual reality and augmented reality systems.
Prerequisite: IAT 343 or IAT 312, with a minimum grade of C-.
