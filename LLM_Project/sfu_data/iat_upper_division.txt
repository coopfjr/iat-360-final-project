SFU SIAT Upper Division Course Offerings and Prerequisites (300 and 400 Level)

IAT 309W (Writing Methods for Research): Develops critical thinking and writing strategies adaptable to professional communications in design, media arts and technology. Fulfills the upper-division Writing (W) requirement.
Prerequisite: IAT 206W with a minimum grade of C- and completion of 48 units.

IAT 312 (Foundations of Game Design): This course examines the discipline of game design through three analytical frameworks: Games as Rules (formal systems), Games as Play (experiential systems), and Games as Culture (social systems).
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 265 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 313 (Narrative and New Media): Explores narrative theory and practices for new media, focusing on interactive storytelling. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 210 or IAT 265 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 320 (Body as Interface): Explores ideas of embodiment through physical computing supporting human relationships with technology. Students construct and analyze contemporary and historical models of bodily interaction through tangible, gestural and/or e-textile computational prototyping, understand physical practices of embodiment, and apply these concepts to representation, design, and the production of embodied interfaces through various. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units.

IAT 333 (Interaction Design Methods): Focuses on user-centered design methods, including ethnographic research, persona development, and usability testing.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 238 with a minimum grade of C- or IAT 235 with a minimum grade of C- completed before the Spring 2025 term.

IAT 334 (Interface Design): Provides an introduction to the art and design of human-computer interfaces, design methods, prototyping and evaluation of user interfaces. Examines issues of interactivity and its relation to human contexts and technological systems.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 235 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 336 (Materials in Practice): Introduces material properties and performance in the context of physical and digital interactive artifacts. Develops practices of material selection and use, including 3D printing and digital fabrication methods.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 102 and IAT 106, both with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 338 (Interactive Objects and Environments): Explores the design of physical interactive objects and embedded computing environments.
Prerequisite: Completion of 75 units, including IAT 309W and IAT 333, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 339 (Web Design and Development): Advanced theory and implementation of web media from a design perspective. Students will design and develop web media that focuses on communication and design issues for a variety of users.
Prerequisite: IAT 235 and IAT 265, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 340 (Sound Design): Principles and techniques of sound design for media and interactive systems.
Prerequisite: Completion of 24 units. Recommended: IAT 202.

IAT 343 (Animation): Theory and practice of 3D computer animation. Introduces the principles of 3D computer animation, including modeling, rigging, and keyframing.
Prerequisite: Minimum of 24 units, including MATH 130 or MACM 101 or MATH 150 or MATH 151 or MATH 154 or MATH 157, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 344 (Moving Images): Reviews and consolidates the fundamentals of digital video production, including camera and composition skills, the role of sound, lighting, and continuity and montage editing.
Prerequisite: Minimum of 48 units and IAT 202 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 351 (Advanced Human-Computer Interaction): Focuses on advanced human-computer interaction concepts, building upon foundations in cognition and interface design.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 201 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 352 (Internet Computing Technologies): XML technologies, databases and data mining are reviewed as means of storing and extracting knowledge. Server-client and service oriented architectures are examined from the perspective of building interactive systems. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 235, and either IAT 265 or other approved second year programming course.

IAT 355 (Introduction to Visual Analytics): Techniques for analyzing and visualizing complex data sets.
Prerequisite: IAT 201 and either IAT 265 or CMPT 225, all with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 359 (Mobile Computing): An introduction to mobile computing and the development of applications for mobile environments. Topics covered include mobile development environments, user interfaces, and user experience.
Prerequisite: Completion of 48 units, including IAT 265 with a minimum grade of C-. Students with credit for CMPT 362 may not take this course for further credit.

IAT 360 (Exploring Artificial Intelligence: Its Use, Concepts, and Impact): Designed to provide a comprehensive and accessible introduction to the world of artificial intelligence that will empower the students to navigate the AI-driven future. Students will explore fundamental AI concepts, including machine learning, neural networks, natural language processing, and computer vision; discover real-world applications, ethical considerations, and the societal impact of AI.
Prerequisite: CMPT 120, IAT 265, and IAT 267, all with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 380 (Special Topics in Interactive Arts and Technology (Arts)): Specific details of courses to be offered will be published prior to registration each semester. This course can be repeated for credit up to a maximum of three times, if topic studied is different. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 45 units.

IAT 381 (Special Topics in Interactive Arts and Technology (Science)): Special topics in Interactive Arts and Technology. Prerequisite: Completion of 45 units.

IAT 386 (Directed Studies): Independent study project under the supervision of a faculty member.
Prerequisite: Completion of 45 units, and permission of the instructor and of the school. No more than two directed studies may be used towards the IAT major requirements.

IAT 410 (Advanced Game Design): An advanced project-based course where students work in teams to design and develop a fully playable digital game.
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units, including IAT 265 and IAT 312, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 431 (Speculative Design): Provides students with the opportunity to experiment with designing in various non-normative frameworks provided by cultural studies, critical theory and phenomenology. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 72 units, including IAT 333 or IAT 334, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 432 (Design Evaluation): Examines evaluation concepts and methods for designers. Introduces a range of evaluation approaches including informal methods, controlled experiments, and usability studies.
Prerequisite: Completion of 72 units, including IAT 333 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 437 (Representation and Fabrication): Introduces computer-based tools for representing and fabricating designs. Covers the representation of work within a design process, the use of visualization techniques, and the use of digital fabrication technology (such as laser cutters and 3D printers).
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units and IAT 336 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 438 (User Experience Design): Advanced level course that examines design practice for Interaction and User Experience Designers. Students apply professional industry standards to related strategies from Graphic Design, Interaction Design, and UI Design.
Prerequisite: Completion of 72 units, including IAT 338 or permission of the instructor.

IAT 443 (Senior Project in Creative Media): A senior level investigation of skills and knowledge learned across creative media concentration. Examines recombinant, computational and compositional structures related to image, sound, video and/or motion capture.
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units, including IAT 313, IAT 340, and IAT 344, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 445 (Immersive Environments): Explores the design and development of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) experiences.
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units, including IAT 343 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 452 (Developing Evidence-Based Interfaces): Introduces methodological approaches to theory-informed and evidence-driven design of multimodal interactive interfaces for software systems to be employed in dynamic environments.
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units, including IAT 351 with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 455 (Computational Media): The representation of media is introduced: specifically one dimensional (sound), two dimensional (images) and three dimensional (moving images). This course focuses on techniques and methods for creating digital video special effects.
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units, including IAT 265 and MACM 101, with a minimum grade of C-.

IAT 480 (Special Topics in Interactive Arts and Technology (Arts)): Specific details of courses to be offered will be published prior to enrollment each term. This course can be repeated for credit up to a maximum of three times, if topic studied is different. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units.

IAT 481 (Special Topics in Interactive Arts and Technology (Science)): Special topics in Interactive Arts and Technology. Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units.

IAT 486 (Directed Studies): Independent reading and research topics selected in consultation with individual members of the SIAT faculty. This course can be repeated for credit up to a maximum of three times, if topic studied is different. No more than two directed studies may be used towards the IAT major requirements. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 69 units, and permission of the instructor and of the school are required.

IAT 487 (Directed Studies): Independent reading and research topics. Prerequisite: Completion of 60 units.

IAT 488 (Directed Studies): Independent reading and research topics selected in consultation with individual members of the IAT faculty. This course can be repeated for credit up to a maximum of three times, if topic studied is different. 
Prerequisite: Completion of 69 units. No more than six units of Directed Studies may be taken, and permission of the instructor and school are required.

IAT 490 (Honours Project I): Research work related to a particular topic in the field of Interactive Arts and Technology. Students will undertake an individual project under supervision of a SIAT faculty member. 
Prerequisite: Students accepted into Honours Program and project accepted by a supervisor.

IAT 491 (Honours Project II): Research work related to a particular topic in the field of Interactive Arts and Technology. Students will undertake an individual project under supervision of a SIAT faculty member. 
Prerequisite: Students accepted into Honours Program and project accepted by a supervisor.
