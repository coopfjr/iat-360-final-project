SFU Academic Regulations: Course Loads and Credit Limits
Simon Fraser University considers any undergraduate student enrolled in nine units (credits) or more per term as a full-time student. Students enrolled in one to eight units per term are considered part-time.
For students in the Faculty of Communication, Art and Technology (which includes SIAT), the recommended course load for students in good academic standing is 15 units per term.
The absolute maximum course load permitted in a single term is 18 units. 
Students may not exceed 18 units in a single term unless they complete a 'Course Overload' form and are granted explicit permission by their faculty advisors (e.g., FASS or FCAT advising). 
During the shorter Summer Session or Intersession, students may not enroll in a program of more than 10 units of work.
