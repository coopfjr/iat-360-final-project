SIAT Concentrations (Updated for 2026 Academic Calendar)
A concentration is an area of specialization that approved SIAT majors can pursue within their Bachelor's degree. Students may complete more than one concentration, or choose to complete none.

Current Active Concentrations (Effective Fall 2024 and Fall 2025 onward):
* AI and Data Science for Human-Centred Systems: Must complete IAT 355, IAT 360, IAT 460, and IAT 461.
* Creative Media: Must complete IAT 313, IAT 340, IAT 344, and IAT 443.
* Critical Making: Must complete IAT 336, IAT 320, and IAT 420.
* Design and Development for Web & Mobile: Must complete IAT 339, IAT 359, and IAT 459.
* Designing Interactions: Must complete IAT 333, IAT 431, and IAT 438.
* Evidence-Based Interactive Systems: Must complete IAT 334, IAT 351, IAT 432, and IAT 452.
* Extended Reality & Game Design: Must complete IAT 312, IAT 343, IAT 410, and IAT 445.

Legacy / Historical Concentrations (Phased out for new admissions as of Fall 2024, but existing students may still complete them):
* Design Concentration (Historical): Must complete six of the following: IAT 333, IAT 334, IAT 336, IAT 339, IAT 431, IAT 432, IAT 437, or IAT 438.
* Media Arts Concentration (Historical): Must complete IAT 313, IAT 340, IAT 344, and IAT 443.
* Interactive Systems Concentration (Historical): Must complete six of the following: IAT 351, IAT 352, IAT 355, IAT 359, IAT 410, IAT 452, or IAT 455.
* Game Design Concentration (Historical): Includes IAT 312, IAT 343, IAT 410, and IAT 445.
