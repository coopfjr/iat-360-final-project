SIAT Concentrations (Effective Fall 2024 onward)
A concentration is an area of specialization that approved SIAT majors can pursue. Students may complete more than one concentration, or choose to complete none. 
The available concentrations and their required courses are:
Media Arts Concentration: Must complete IAT 313, IAT 340, IAT 344, and IAT 443.
Design Concentration: Must complete six of the following: IAT 333, IAT 334, IAT 336, IAT 339, IAT 431, IAT 432, IAT 437, or IAT 438.
Interactive Systems Concentration: Must complete six of the following: IAT 351, IAT 352, IAT 355, IAT 359, IAT 410, IAT 452, or IAT 455.
Game Design Concentration (Historical): Includes IAT 312, IAT 343, IAT 410, and IAT 445.
Note: As of Fall 2024, the specific Design, Media Arts, and Interactive Systems concentrations are no longer available to newly admitted students, but existing students may still complete them.
