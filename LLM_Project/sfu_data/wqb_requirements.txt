SFU WQB Requirements (Writing, Quantitative, and Breadth)
All students completing an undergraduate bachelor's degree program at Simon Fraser University must complete courses that are formally designated W, Q, and B with a minimum grade of C- or better.

Writing (W) Requirements:
Students must complete two W courses (minimum three units each) for a total of 6 units.
At least one of these W courses must be an upper-division course taken at SFU within the student's major subject.

Quantitative (Q) Requirements:
Students must complete two Q courses (total six units or more).
Q courses may be either lower or upper division. Students are advised to complete their first Q course within their first 60 units. 

Breadth (B) Requirements:
Students must complete a total of 24 units of breadth courses.
Designated Breadth (18 units): Must be outside the student's major subject. This includes:
- Two courses (6 units) of Social Sciences (B-Soc)
- Two courses (6 units) of Humanities (B-Hum)
- Two courses (6 units) of Sciences (B-Sci)
Additional Breadth (6 units): Two courses (total six units or more) outside the student's major subject. These may or may not be B-designated courses.

Second Degrees: 
Students admitted to a second degree program are exempted from all breadth requirements, three units of the Q requirements, and the lower division W requirement. They must still complete one upper-division W course in their major and one 3-unit Q course.

External Course Verification:
SFU offers hundreds of WQB-certified courses across various faculties. If a student asks about a specific non-SIAT elective or external course that is not explicitly detailed in this dataset, explain that while you know the general WQB rules, they should verify whether that specific external course carries a W, Q, or B designation by checking the official SFU WQB Certified Courses database or the current SFU Academic Calendar.
